About me

Community
at the core

A community-first approach to program design, student support, and storytelling.

I'm a Community Engagement Specialist at the Fleet Science Center, where I design and manage programs that connect underserved San Diego neighborhoods — particularly National City and San Ysidro — with science, learning, and each other.

My work lives at the intersection of relationship-building, program management, and creative communication. Whether I'm coordinating a STEM fair for hundreds of families, recruiting scientists for community events, or writing grant reports, I bring the same thing: genuine care for the people I'm serving and an eye for what actually works in the real world.

Outside of work, I explore freelance branding and design, and I'm always looking for ways to grow — professionally and creatively.
